    \textbf{Decision Making and Elastic Scalability Management in Heterogeneous Cloud Environments Based on Workload Prediction}

    \vspace{0.5cm}

    \textbf{Muhammad Zahid Masruri / NRP 6025222041}

    \vspace{0.5cm}

    Modern cloud architecture has transformed the way web applications are developed, used, and managed, offering significant flexibility, scalability, and efficiency. This evolution includes a shift from traditional infrastructure such as virtual machines to container technology and serverless models. Kubernetes, a container-based cluster management system, is popular for its ability to scale on demand. However, traditional scalability algorithms often fail to handle sudden traffic spikes, resulting in increased latency and inefficient resource allocation. As a solution, serverless computing or Function as a Service (FaaS) provides automatic scalability and cost efficiency by charging only based on actual usage, though this model incurs high costs for constant workloads.

    This research explores the integration between Kubernetes and serverless computing to optimize the performance and cost efficiency of cloud applications. While several studies have focused on resource allocation prediction, challenges in managing traffic spikes and dynamic resource allocation remain unresolved. This study proposes a traffic distribution strategy utilizing GRU-based workload prediction and a resource reservation mechanism to ensure optimal allocation and fulfillment of tail latency. By combining the strengths of Kubernetes and serverless, this research aims to create a more resilient, responsive, and cost-efficient cloud architecture.

    \vspace{0.5cm}

    \textbf{Keywords:} \emph{Cloud Computing, Gated Recurrent Unit, Serverless, Function as A Service, Container, Kubernetes}
