### 1a. Criteria from IF-InfoTesis (verbatim meaning, Indonesian source)

Ketentuan Publikasi Ilmiah S2 Teknik Informatika:

1. Counts only if **accepted** (journal) or **presented** (seminar) while an active S2 IF ITS student.
2. Topic must match the thesis topic. It may be done before the proposal defense.
3. Venue type needs the adviser's knowledge and approval.
4. Student is **first author**. Adviser is a **co-author**.
5. Journal: adviser **must be corresponding author**. International seminar: adviser or student may be corresponding author.
6. Allowed types, valid for the publication grade and yudisium:
   - Presented at a **reputable international seminar**: indexed, more than 5 countries, proceedings indexed in Scopus or Web of Science.
   - Article in a **SINTA-accredited national journal** or a **Scopus-indexed international journal**, proven by an **LoA or "Accepted" notice**.
7. Scholarship holders (LPDP, BPI, FGB, FT, PMDSU, etc.) must publish in a Scopus-indexed international journal.
8. Documents: article file; LoA (journal) or presentation documentation plus presenter certificate (seminar); form "Pernyataan Telah Melakukan Publikasi Ilmiah" signed by student, adviser, Kaprodi; form "Pernyataan Bersedia Menyerahkan Jurnal Apabila Sudah Terbit" with materai, only if the journal is not yet published.
9. Kaprodi signature (Pak Hudan) via https://its.id/TandatanganKaprodi. Templates: https://its.id/FolderTesisS2IF. Submit at https://its.id/PublikasiIlmiahS2IF.

**Grading table (Kriteria Penilaian):**

| Type | Mahasiswa Lama | Mahasiswa Baru (intake Gasal 2025/2026) |
|---|---|---|
| International journal | Q1-Q4: A | Q1-Q2: A; Q3-Q4: AB |
| National journal | SINTA 1-2: A; SINTA 3-4: AB | SINTA 1-2 + Scopus Q1-Q2: A; SINTA 1-2 + Scopus Q3-Q4: AB; SINTA 1-2 not Scopus: AB; SINTA 3-4: B |
| International seminar | CORE rank B or above: A; below B: AB; not in CORE: B | same |

Check links from the page: SINTA https://sinta.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/, Scopus https://www.scopus.com/sources, CORE https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/.

Sidang akhir items on the same page: draft thesis at least 60 pages (Bab 1-5), logbook at least 6 meetings, pra-tesis seminar with at least 6 attendees, foreign-language certificate.
